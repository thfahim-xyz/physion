# Fundamental Concepts of Thermodynamics

## Thermodynamic System

A thermodynamic system is a body and/or radiation separate from its surroundings that can be studeied using the laws of thermodynamics. A thermodynamic system can be isolated, closed or open.

**Isolated System:** No exchange of matter or energy with its surroundings.

**Closed System:** Exchanges energy with its surroundings, but not matter.

**Open System:** Exchanges both energy and matter with its surroundings.
