# Thermal Conduction

## Heat Flux Density

Heat flux density $\mathbf q$ is the heat current per unit cross-sectional area.

## Heat Current

Heat current (AKA heat flux) $\Phi_q$ id the heat transfer rate through a surface.

$$
    \boxed{\Phi_q = \int \mathbf q \cdot \mathrm d \mathbf S} \implies \Phi_q = \frac{\mathrm d Q}{\mathrm d t}
$$

## Thermal Conductivity

Thermal conductivity $k$ is the ability of an object to conduct heat.

## Heat Conduction

$$
    \mathbf q = -k \nabla T
$$

$$
    Q = - k \frac{A \Delta t}{L} \Delta T
$$

where
- $k$ is the thermal conductivity
- $\Delta t$ is the time interval
- $L$ is the length between two ends
- $A$ is the cross-sectional area
- $\Delta T$ is the temperature difference

## Thermal Diffusivity

$$
    a = \frac{k}{\rho c_p}
$$

where
- $k$ is the thermal conductivity
- $\rho$ is the density of the medium
- $c_p$ is the isobaric specific heat capacity

## Heat Equation

$$
    \boxed{\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = a \nabla^2 T}
$$
