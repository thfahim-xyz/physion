# Particle Mechanics

Particle mechanics is the study of the motion of particles. It is traditionally divided into particle kinematics and particle dynamics.

Particle kinematics is the study of the motion of particles without considering the forces or interactions responsible for that motion. It provides the mathematical framework for describing a particle's position, displacement, velocity, and acceleration, and for relating these quantities to one another.

Kinematics forms the foundation of classical mechanics: before asking why a particle moves, we first need a precise description of how it moves.

Particle dynamics is the study of the motion of particles and the causes of that motion. While kinematics describes how a particle moves, dynamics relates its motion to forces and interactions.

Particle dynamics provides the foundation for analysing systems ranging from simple projectiles and pendulums to interacting particles and orbital motion. It also introduces the concepts of momentum, force, inertia, and the equations of motion that determine how a system evolves in time.

## Prerequisites

A basic understanding of the following is useful:

- Scalars and vectors
- Coordinate systems
- Elementary algebra and trigonometry
- Differentiation and integration of single-variable functions
- Basic concepts of position, time, and reference frames
