# Rotational Kinematic Quantities

## Angular Displacement

Angular displacement is the angle through which the body has rotated (revolved or spun) around a center of rotation or axis of rotation. Counterclockwise rotation is considered positive displacement and clockwise rotation is considered negative displacement.

$$
    \boxed{\boldsymbol \theta = \theta \, \hat{\mathbf n}}
$$

## Angular Velocity

Angular velocity is the rate of change of an angular coordinate with respect to time.

$$
    \boxed{\boldsymbol \omega = \dot{\theta} \, \hat{\mathbf n}}
$$

It is associated with rotational or orbital motion.

## Angular Acceleration

Rate of change of angular velocity with respect to time.

$$
    \boxed{\boldsymbol \alpha = \dot{\boldsymbol \omega} = \ddot{\theta} \, \hat{\mathbf n}}
$$
