# Central Force Dynamics

Central force dynamics studies the motion of particles under forces directed along the line joining the particle to a fixed centre, with magnitude depending only on the distance from that centre.

Central force dynamics forms the foundation for understanding planetary orbits, gravitational and electrostatic motion, scattering, and the classical two-body problem.

## Prerequisites

A basic understanding of the following is useful:

- Particle kinematics
- Particle dynamics
- Systems of particles
- Newton's laws of motion
- Vectors and vector algebra
- Momentum and angular momentum
- Work, energy, and conservative forces
- Differential and integral calculus
