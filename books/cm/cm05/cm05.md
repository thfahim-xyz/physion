# Oscillations and Waves

## Oscillations and Waves

Oscillations and waves describe the repeated or propagating motion of physical systems about an equilibrium state. An oscillation occurs when a system undergoes bounded, typically periodic motion, while a wave describes the propagation of a disturbance through space and time. Waves extend these ideas to systems with many coupled degrees of freedom. Their behaviour is described by quantities such as wavelength, frequency, phase, and wave speed.

The subject provides a common framework for mechanical vibrations, sound, electromagnetic waves, and many other physical phenomena.

## Prerequisites

A basic understanding of the following is useful:

- Particle kinematics
- Newton's laws of motion
- Differential equations
- Complex numbers and trigonometry
- Energy and momentum
- Basic concepts of fields and continuous systems
