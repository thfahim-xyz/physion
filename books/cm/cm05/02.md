## Wave Anatomy

### Waveform

This is the shape of a wave's graph.

The side-view of any wave is called a wave profile and it shows the amplitude, wavelength and other characteristics of the wave.

### Wave Envelop

A smooth curve outlining a wave's extremes. It generalizes constant amplitude to an instantaneous amplitude envelop.

### Wavefront

Set of all points in a wave field having the same phase.

## Wave Function

$$
    \Psi \equiv \Psi (\mathbf r, t)
$$

## Phase

Phase is an angle that defines the precise state of motion of an oscillating object at any given time.

For a plane wave,

$$
    \phi (\mathbf{r},t) = \mathbf{k} \cdot \mathbf{r} - \omega t + \phi_0
$$

where $\phi_0$ is the initial phase angle. The negative sign means that the wave propagates in the direction of $\mathbf{r}$.

## Amplitude

$$
    \boxed{A = \max |\Psi (\mathbf r, t)|}
$$

## Wave Vector

$$
    \boxed{\mathbf k = \nabla \phi}
$$

The magnitude of the wave vector is the wavenumber.

$$
    \boxed{k = |\mathbf k|}
$$

## Frequency

Frequency is the rate at which an oscillatory system oscillates in time.

For a wave,

$$
    \boxed{\omega = -\dfrac{\partial \phi}{\partial t}}
$$

The negative sign is there by convention.

There is also the linear frequency.

$$
    f = \dfrac{\omega}{2 \pi}
$$

## Period

Period is the smallest number $T$ such that

$$
    \phi(\mathbf r, t + T) - \phi(\mathbf{r}, t) = 2 \pi
$$

## Wavelength

Wavelength $\lambda$ is the magnitude of the vector $\boldsymbol \lambda$ such that

$$
    \phi(\mathbf r + \boldsymbol \lambda, t) - \phi(\mathbf r, t) = 2 \pi
$$

## Phase Velocity

Phase velocity is the velocity of individual oscillations.

$$
    \boxed{\mathbf v_p = \dfrac{\omega}{k} \mathbf{\hat k}}
$$

## Group Velocity

Group velocity is the velocity of the wave packet.

$$
    \boxed{\mathbf{v}_g = \nabla_{\mathbf k} \omega}
$$

Group velocity only exists when the medium is dispersive.

## Path Length

Physical distance traveled by a wave from the source point to any point in space.

$$
    s = \int_C |\mathrm d \mathbf r|
$$

where $\mathrm d \mathbf r$ satisfies

$$
    \mathrm d \phi = \mathbf k \cdot \mathrm d \mathbf r
$$

## Wave Equation

$$
    \boxed{\dfrac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial t^2} = v_p^2 \nabla^2 \Psi}
$$

General Solution:

$$
    \boxed{\Psi (\mathbf r,t) = \int A(\mathbf{k}) 
    e^{i(\mathbf k \cdot \mathbf r - \omega t)} d^3 k}
$$

which is a superposition of plane waves.

Monochromatic Plane Wave Solution:

$$
    \boxed{\Psi (\mathbf{r}, t) = Ae^{i(\mathbf{k} \cdot \mathbf{r} - \omega t)}}
$$

## Dispersion Relation

$$
    \omega = \omega(\mathbf k) = v_p k
$$

## Equations of Motion of a Harmonic Wave

$$
    \boxed{f = \dfrac{N}{t}}
$$

$$
    \boxed{T = \dfrac{t}{N} = \dfrac{1}{f}}
$$

$$
    \boxed{\omega = \dfrac{2 \pi N}{t} = 2 \pi f}
$$
