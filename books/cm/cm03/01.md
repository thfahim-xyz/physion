# Energy Quantities

## Work

In general, work is the change in energy of any physical system.

$$
    \boxed{W = \Delta E}
$$

Work is the energy transferred to or from an object via the application of force along a displacement.

$$
    \boxed{W = \int_C \mathbf F \cdot \mathrm d \mathbf r}
$$

Work done against a force field $(W_{ON})$ gives energy to the system. Work done by a force field $(W_{BY})$ takes away energy from the system.

$$
    W_{ON} = -W_{BY}
$$

## Energy

Energy is the fundamental capacity of an object or a system to do work or to cause change. Energy can exist in various forms such as kinetic energy, potential energy, mass energy, mechanical energy, thermal energy, electrical energy, radiant energy, etc. 

### Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the form of energy that an object possesses due to its motion.

$$
    \boxed{K = \dfrac{p^2}{2m} = \dfrac{1}{2} m v^2}
$$

### Potential Energy

Potential energy is the stored energy an object or system possesses due to its position, arrangement, or state.

It is not possible to directly measure the potential energy of a system in an absolute sense, since potential energy is a relative quantity defined by an arbitrary reference point. Only changes in potential energy $(\Delta U)$  are physically meaningful and measurable.

$$
    \boxed{\Delta U = -W}
$$

Potential energy only exists if the force is conservative. Every conservative force has potential energy and a potential energy function associated with it.

$$
    \mathbf F = -\nabla U
$$

Work done against a force field $(W_{ON})$ increases the potential energy of the system (i.e., gives energy to the system). Work done by a force field $(W_{BY})$ decreases the potential energy of the system (i.e., takes away energy from the system).

$$
\begin{gather*}
    W_{ON} \implies U \; \text{increases} \\
    W_{BY} \implies U \; \text{decreases}
\end{gather*}
$$

By following two principles, one can consistently assign a non-relative value to $U$, in which case, $\Delta U$ becomes $U(\mathbf r)$.

- Wherever $F = 0$, $U \equiv 0$ as well.

- Whenever $F$ does work, potential energy is lost $(\Delta U < 0)$.

## Power

Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred.

$$
    \boxed{P = \dot E}
$$
