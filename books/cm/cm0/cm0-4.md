# {{ page.title }}

The net torque acting on an object is proportional to the net angular acceleration of that object.

$$
    \boxed{\boldsymbol \tau_{net} = I \boldsymbol \alpha_{net}}
$$

where

$$
    \boldsymbol \tau_{net} = \sum \boldsymbol \tau
$$
