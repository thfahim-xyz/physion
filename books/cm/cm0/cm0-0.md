# {{ page.title }}

Elementary mechanics is the introductory study of motion, forces, energy, and momentum, focusing on the fundamental principles that govern the behavior of objects in everyday situations. It develops the basic concepts needed to describe how bodies move, interact, and respond to applied forces, often through simple systems such as projectiles, inclined planes, pulleys, and collisions. Emphasizing clear physical reasoning alongside essential mathematics, it forms the first step toward deeper studies in classical mechanics and physics as a whole.
