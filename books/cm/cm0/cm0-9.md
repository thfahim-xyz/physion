# {{ page.title }}

For a rotating rigid body,

$$
    \mathbf v = \mathbf v_t = \boldsymbol \omega \times \mathbf r
$$

$$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf a &= \mathbf a_c + \mathbf a_t \\  
        &= \boldsymbol \omega \times \mathbf v_t + \boldsymbol \alpha \times \mathbf r
    \end{aligned}
$$
