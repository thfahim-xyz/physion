## Gravitational Field

$$
    \boxed{\mathbf g = - \nabla \phi_g}
$$

For a massive particle,

$$
    \boxed{\mathbf g = - \dfrac{GM}{r^2} \mathbf{\hat r}}
$$

## Gravitational Potential

## Gravitational Flux

$$
    \boxed{\Phi_g = \oint_{\partial V} \mathbf g \cdot \mathrm d \mathbf A 
            = \iiint_V \nabla \cdot \mathbf g \, \mathrm dV}
$$

## Escape Speed

$$
    \boxed{v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}}
$$
