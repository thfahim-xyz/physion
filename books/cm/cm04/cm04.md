# Rigid Body Dynamics

Rigid body dynamics studies the motion of extended bodies whose internal distances remain constant. Unlike a particle, a rigid body can undergo both translational and rotational motion, requiring a description of its orientation as well as the motion of its centre of mass.

The subject provides the framework for analysing the motion of bodies such as wheels, discs, pendulums, and rotating mechanical systems. It also introduces the inertia tensor, principal axes, and the general rotational dynamics of three-dimensional rigid bodies.

## Prerequisites

A basic understanding of the following is useful:

- Particle kinematics
- Particle dynamics
- Systems of particles
- Vectors and vector algebra
- Torque and angular momentum
- Differentiation and integration
