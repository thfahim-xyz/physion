# {{ page.title }}

# Generalized Coordinates

For a particle,

$$
    (\mathbf q, \dot{\mathbf q}, t)
$$

# Lagrangian

For a particle,

$$
    L(\mathbf q, \dot{\mathbf q}, t) = T(\mathbf q, \dot{\mathbf q}) - V(\mathbf q, t)
$$

# Action

$$
    S[\mathbf q] = \int_{t_0}^{t_1} L(\mathbf q, \dot{\mathbf q}, t) \, \mathrm d t
$$

# Principle of Stationary Action

$$
    \boxed{\delta S = 0}
$$

# Euler-Lagrange Equation

$$
    \dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \mathbf q} = \dfrac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm d t} \left( \dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{\mathbf q}} \right)
$$
