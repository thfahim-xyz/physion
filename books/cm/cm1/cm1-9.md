## Simple Harmonic Oscillator

Forces:

$$
\begin{gather*}
    F_s = -kx \\
    F_{net} = -kx
\end{gather*}
$$

Equation of motion:

$$
    \boxed{\ddot x + \omega^2 x = 0}
$$

where

$$
    \omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}
$$

Solutions:

$$
\begin{gather*}
    x(t) = A \sin (\omega t + \varphi) \quad (-A \leq x \leq A) \\
    v = \omega \sqrt{A^2 - x^2} = A\omega \cos (\omega t + \varphi) \quad (0 \leq v \leq A \omega) \\
    a = - \omega^2 x = -A \omega^2 \sin (\omega t + \varphi) \quad (-A \omega^2 \leq x \leq A \omega^2)
\end{gather*}
$$

Energy of the oscillator:

$$
\begin{gather*}
    K = \dfrac{1}{2} m \omega^2 (A^2 - x^2) \quad (0 \leq K \leq E) \\
    U = \dfrac{1}{2} m \omega^2 x^2 \quad (0 \leq U \leq E) \\
    E = \dfrac{1}{2} m \omega^2 A^2 \quad (E = constant)
\end{gather*}
$$

## Damped Harmonic Oscillator

Forces:

$$
\begin{gather*}
    f = - bv \qquad \text{(Damping force)} \\
    F_s = -kx \\
    F_{net} = -kx -bv
\end{gather*}
$$

Equation of motion:

$$
\boxed{\ddot x + \gamma \dot x + \omega_0^2 x = 0}
$$

where

$$
\begin{gather*}
    \boxed{\gamma = \dfrac{b}{m}} \\
    \boxed{\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}}
\end{gather*}
$$

Solutions:

$$
\begin{gather*}
    \boxed{x(t) = Ae^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}t} \sin (\omega t + \varphi)} \\
    \boxed{\omega = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \dfrac{\gamma^2}{4}}} \\
    \boxed{\lambda = \dfrac{\gamma}{2}} \\
    \boxed{\tau = \dfrac{1}{\gamma}}
\end{gather*}
$$

Energy of the oscillator:

$$
\begin{gather*}
    \boxed{E(t) = E_0 e^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}t} = \dfrac{1}{2} m \omega^2 A^2 e^{-\frac{\gamma}{2}t}} \\
    \boxed{E_0 = \dfrac{1}{2} m \omega^2 A^2} \\
    \boxed{\dfrac{\mathrm d E}{\mathrm d t} = -\gamma E}
\end{gather*}
$$

Damping conditions:

$$
\zeta = \dfrac{\gamma}{2 \omega_0} = \dfrac{b}{2 \sqrt{mk}}
$$

$$
    \zeta < 1 \implies \text{Underdamped} \\
    \zeta = 1 \implies \text{Critically damped} \\
    \zeta > 1 \implies \text{Overdamped} \\
$$
