## Wave

### Waveform

This is the shape of a wave's graph.

The side-view of any wave is called a wave profile and it shows the amplitude, wavelength and other characteristics of the wave.

### Wave Envelop

A smooth curve outlining a wave's extremes. It generalizes constant amplitude to an instantaneous amplitude envelop.

### Wavefront

Set of all points in a wave field having the same phase.
