# {{ page.title }}

$$
    \boxed{\dfrac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial t^2} = v_p^2 \nabla^2 \Psi}
$$

General Solution:

$$
    \boxed{\Psi (\mathbf r,t) = \int A(\mathbf{k}) 
    e^{i(\mathbf k \cdot \mathbf r - \omega t)} d^3 k}
$$

which is a superposition of plane waves.

Monochromatic Plane Wave Solution:

$$
    \boxed{\Psi (\mathbf{r}, t) = Ae^{i(\mathbf{k} \cdot \mathbf{r} - \omega t)}}
$$
