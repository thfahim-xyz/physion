# Systems of Particles

A system of particles is a collection of particles considered together as a single physical system. Its motion can often be described in terms of collective quantities such as the centre of mass, total momentum, angular momentum, and total energy.

The dynamics of the system can then be separated into the motion of its centre of mass and motion relative to the centre of mass. This provides a powerful framework for studying collisions, explosions, rigid bodies, and other many-particle systems.

The concepts developed here extend single-particle mechanics to systems in which particles interact with one another, providing the foundation for more advanced topics in classical mechanics.

## Prerequisites

A basic understanding of the following is useful:

- Particle kinematics
- Particle dynamics
- Newton's laws of motion
- Vectors and vector algebra
- Momentum and conservation laws
- Basic differentiation and integration
