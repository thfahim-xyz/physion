# {{ page.title }}

# Generalized Coordinates

$$
    (\mathbf q, \mathbf p, t)
$$

# Generalized Momentum

$$
    \mathbf p = \dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{\mathbf q}}
$$

# Hamiltonian

For a particle,

$$
    H(\mathbf q, \mathbf p, t) = \mathbf p \dot{\mathbf q} - L(\mathbf q, \dot{\mathbf q}, t)
$$

# Hamilton's Equations

For a particle,

$$
    \dot{\mathbf p} = - \dfrac{\partial H}{\partial \mathbf q}
$$

$$
    \dot{\mathbf q} = \dfrac{\partial H}{\partial \mathbf p}
$$
