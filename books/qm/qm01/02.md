## Matter Waves

Matter Wave is the idea that particles such as electrons also behave like waves. Louis de Broglie proposed that every particle has a wavelength given by

$$
    \lambda = \dfrac{h}{p} \implies \mathbf p = \hbar \mathbf k
$$

where $\mathbf p = \gamma m \mathbf v$ is the relativistic momentum and $\mathbf k$ is the angular wave vector.

## Double-slit Experiment

Double-slit experiment demonstrates wave-particle duality. Particles like electrons produce an interference pattern when passing through two slits, showing wave-like behavior even when sent one at a time.

## Compton Scattering

Compton scattering is the increase in wavelength of X-rays or photons after colliding with electrons. It provided strong evidence that light carries momentum and behaves as particles.

$$
    \lambda^{\prime} - \lambda = \dfrac{h}{m_e c}(1 - \cos \theta)
$$

where $\lambda^{\prime}$ is the wavelength after scattering and $\theta$ is the scattering angle.
