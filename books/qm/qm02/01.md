## Quantized Blackbody Radiation

Blackbody radiation is the emission of electromagnetic radiation by a hot object. Max Planck explained it by proposing that energy is emitted in discrete packets called quanta, leading to the birth of quantum mechanics.

$$
    E = h \nu = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda} = \hbar \omega
$$

where $\nu$ is the frequency, $\lambda$ is the wavelength and $\omega$ is the angular frequency of the wave.

## Photoelectric Effect

Photoelectric effect is the emission of electrons from a metal surface when light shines on it. Albert Einstein explained it by showing that light consists of particles called photons with quantized energy.

$$
    K_{max} = h \nu - W
$$

$$
    W = h \nu_0 \implies K_{max} = h (\nu - \nu_0)
$$

Photoelectric effect occurs when $\nu > \nu_0$, where $\nu$ is the frequency of the incident photon and $\nu_0$ is the threshold frequency of the material. $W$ is the work function.
