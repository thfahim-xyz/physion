# Magnetic Potential

## Work and Energy in a Magnetic Field

Work done by a magnet is

$$
    W = \int \mathbf B \cdot \mathrm d \mathbf m
$$

where $\mathbf B$ is the external magnetic field.

### Magnetic Potential Energy

Potential energy of a magnet is

$$
    U_m = - W
$$

### Magnetic Field Energy Density

Potential energy density stored in a magnetic field is

$$
    u_m = \dfrac{1}{2} \dfrac{|\mathbf B|^2}{\mu_0} = \dfrac{1}{2} \, \mathbf J \cdot \mathbf A
$$

## Magnetic Vector Potential

It is defined by the following equation:

$$
    \mathbf B = \nabla \times \mathbf A
$$
