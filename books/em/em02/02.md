# Magnetic Potential

## Magnetic Vector Potential

It is defined by the following equation:

$$
    \mathbf B = \nabla \times \mathbf A
$$

## Gauss's Law for Magnetism

The net magnetic flux through a closed surface is zero.

$$
    \Phi_B = 0
$$

$$
    \implies \oint_{\partial V} \mathbf B \cdot \mathrm d \mathbf S = 0
$$

$$
    \implies \nabla \cdot \mathbf B = 0
$$

## Poisson's Equation for Magnetostatics

$$
    \nabla \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf A) = 0
$$

## Magnetic Dipoles

### Magnetic Moment

In the Ampèrian loop model, a loop of electric current produces a magnetic field that is similar to that of a dipole.

The magnetic moment is given by

$$
    \mathbf m = I \mathbf S
$$

where $I$ is the electric current and $\mathbf S$ is the directed surface area of the loop.

### Magnetic Field of a Magnetic Dipole

$$
    \mathbf B = \frac{\mu_0}{4 \pi} \dfrac{3 (\mathbf m \cdot \hat{\mathbf r}) \hat{\mathbf r} - \mathbf m}{r^3}
$$

## Magnetic Field Energy

Potential energy density stored in a magnetic field is

$$
    u_m = \dfrac{1}{2} \dfrac{|\mathbf B|^2}{\mu_0} = \dfrac{1}{2} \, \mathbf J \cdot \mathbf A
$$
