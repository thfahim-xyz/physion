# Physical Quantities

## Physical Phenomena

### Physical System

A physical system is any collection of one or more physical objects, with a defined boundary (real or conceptual), chosen for analysis, whose internal interactions and exchanges of energy or matter with the surroundings can be studied using the laws of physics.

Conceptually, a physical system is a portion of the physical universe chosen for analysis. Everything outside the physical system is the surroundings.

**Properties:**

- **Defined Boundary:** Separates the system from its surroundings for analysis. The boundary can be real (container) or conceptual (region of space, selected particles).
- **Collection of Objects:** Contains one or more objects.
- **Interactions Within and Across Boundaries:** Internal interactions define system behavior. Exchanges with surroundings allow application of physical laws.

### Physical Object

A physical object is any identifiable entity in the physical universe that can interact with other entities via the transfer of energy or matter.

Conceptually, a physical object is a distinguishable portion of the physical universe that can be treated as a single entity in an analysis.

**Properties:**

- **Existence:** It exists in some region of space-time, even if that region is very small (point-like particle) or extended (macroscopic body).
- **Identifiability:** It can be distinguished from other entities in the universe.
- **Interactability:** It can exchange energy or matter with other objects.

## Physical Quantity

A physical quantity is a property of a material or system that can be quantified by measurement.

A physical quantity can be expressed as a value which is the algebraic multiplication of a numerical value and a unit of measurement.

$$
    \boxed{q = \{q\} \cdot [q]}
$$

where:
- $q$ is the physical quantity
- $\{q\}$ is the numerical value of $q$
- $[q]$ is the unit of $q$

**Intensive Quantity:** Quantity whose magnitude does not depend on the size of the system or the amount of substance that was measured.

**Extensive Quantity:** Quantity whose magnitude is proportional to the size of the system/substance.
