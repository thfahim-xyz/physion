# About This Site

Physion is a personal project built around a simple idea: to understand physics deeply; not just its equations, but also the ideas, intuition, and structure behind them.

It is a collection of notes shaped by curiosity, reasoning, and a desire to understand how nature works.

## About Me

I’m a learner with a strong interest in physics, mathematics, computer science, and philosophy. I’m particularly interested in understanding the fundamental principles that describe the universe and the reasoning that connects them.

I value clarity, intuition, and logical thinking. To me, physics is more than a subject; it is a way of thinking about reality.
